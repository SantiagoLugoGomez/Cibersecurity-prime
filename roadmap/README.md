# The roadmap

Five phases, about 100 planned weeks of study before the open-ended professional phase. Every week is broken into four fixed sessions with exact readings, exercises, self-checks and a deliverable, so you never sit down wondering what to do.

| Phase | Weeks | Hours (approx.) | Modules |
|---|---|---|---|
| [0. Foundations](phase-0-foundations/README.md) | 18 | 135 | Git and study setup, Linux, Bash, networking, Python, operating systems, Windows and PowerShell, home lab |
| [1. Security fundamentals](phase-1-security-fundamentals/README.md) | 16 | 120 | Principles and threat modeling, applied cryptography, identity and access, web security, network security and monitoring, frameworks and vulnerability management, governance |
| [2. Guided practice](phase-2-guided-practice/README.md) | 20 | 150 | CTF foundations, wargames, web application labs, network and system exploitation, blue team labs |
| [3. Specialization](phase-3-specialization/README.md) | 24 | 180 | Six 2-week track samplers, then a 12-week curriculum in your chosen track |
| [4. Professional level](phase-4-professional/README.md) | Ongoing | | Certification prep plans, portfolio projects, bug bounty, career |

Hours assume the semester pace of about 7.5 hours per week. During breaks you can run two weeks of content per calendar week (see [weekly-plan.md](../weekly-plan.md)).

## The weekly session format

Every week in every phase uses the same four sessions. The format is the same so that the habit becomes automatic.

| Code | Session | Length | What you do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R** | Read and study | 75 min | Read the assigned sections with active notes, then write flashcards for the key ideas |
| **P** | Guided practice | 90 min | Structured exercises with a known answer: labs, levels, drills |
| **D** | Deep practice | 3 h | A longer, open problem: a machine, a build, a tool, an investigation. Ends with a short writeup |
| **E** | English | 60 min | Reading, writing or speaking about the same week's topic, in English |

Each week also lists:

- **Check yourself:** questions to answer from memory at the end of the week, out loud or on paper. If you cannot answer one, that topic goes back into next week's R session.
- **Deliverable:** something concrete you commit to your notes or to this repo. A week without a deliverable is not finished.
- **Stretch (optional):** extra material for a week where you have more time or more interest.

How to run the sessions well, take notes, use flashcards and review is explained in [study-system.md](../study-system.md). Session and weekly review templates are in [templates/](../templates/).

## Rules that keep this sustainable

1. **Do the weeks in order inside a phase.** Later weeks assume earlier ones.
2. **A week can take two calendar weeks.** Exam season, illness and life happen. Never skip a week's deliverable to "catch up"; move the schedule instead.
3. **Check-yourself questions decide progress.** If more than two are shaky, repeat the R session before moving on.
4. **Only practice where you are authorized.** Every practice target in this roadmap is your own lab or a platform built for training. See [legal-and-ethics.md](../legal-and-ethics.md).
5. **Write as you go.** Deep practice always ends with notes good enough to become a [writeup](../writeups/TEMPLATE.md).
