# References

> [!TIP]
> Every "Key references" line throughout `docs/` points here. Entries are grouped by type (law and regulation, standards and RFCs, NIST, FIPS, OWASP, MITRE, CIS, academic and foundational works, books, vendor and tool documentation, reports and frameworks), then alphabetized within each group. Where a citation in a page uses slightly different wording ("Rev. 1" versus "Revision 1", a shortened title), it refers to the same entry here; search this page by keyword (document number, author surname, organization) rather than by the exact phrase in the citing page.

**Verified:** facts, version numbers and URLs on this page were checked against primary sources in October and November 2026. Standards, prices and institutional details change; if you are relying on something specific months or years from now, re-verify it against the organization's current page rather than this snapshot.

## Colombian law and regulation

- Congreso de Colombia. **Ley 1273 de 2009** (Ley de Delitos Informáticos), modifying the Penal Code to create offenses including unauthorized access (art. 269A), obstruction (269B), illicit interception (269C), data damage (269D), malicious software (269E), personal data violation (269F), website spoofing (269G), aggravating circumstances (269H), theft by computer means (269I) and non-consensual transfer of assets (269J). Official text: [secretariasenado.gov.co](http://www.secretariasenado.gov.co/senado/basedoc/ley_1273_2009.html).
- Congreso de Colombia. **Ley 1581 de 2012** (Ley Estatutaria de Protección de Datos Personales). Official text: [secretariasenado.gov.co](http://www.secretariasenado.gov.co/senado/basedoc/ley_1581_2012.html).
- Congreso de Colombia. **Ley 1928 de 2018**, approving the Budapest Convention on Cybercrime for Colombia.
- Congreso de Colombia. **Ley 2502 de 2025**, adding an aggravating circumstance for identity fraud committed with artificial intelligence (modifying art. 296 of the Penal Code).
- Corte Constitucional de Colombia. **Sentencia C-224 de 2019**, reviewing the constitutionality of Ley 1928 de 2018 (Budapest Convention).
- Presidencia de la República de Colombia. **Decreto 1377 de 2013**, partially regulating Ley 1581 de 2012.
- Presidencia de la República de Colombia. **Decreto 338 de 2022**, establishing the national digital security governance model and instances.
- Departamento Nacional de Planeación (DNP). **CONPES 3701 de 2011**, Lineamientos de política para ciberseguridad y ciberdefensa.
- Departamento Nacional de Planeación (DNP). **CONPES 3854 de 2016**, Política Nacional de Seguridad Digital.
- Departamento Nacional de Planeación (DNP). **CONPES 3995 de 2020**, Política Nacional de Confianza y Seguridad Digital.
- Departamento Nacional de Planeación (DNP). **CONPES 4144 de 2025**, policy guidance on the responsible development and adoption of artificial intelligence in Colombia.
- Ministerio de Tecnologías de la Información y las Comunicaciones (MinTIC). **Resolución 02277 de 2025**, updating the Modelo de Seguridad y Privacidad de la Información (MSPI) previously set by Resolución 500 de 2021.
- Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia. **Circular Externa 007 de 2018**, minimum requirements for information security and cybersecurity risk management for supervised entities.
- Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia. **Circular Externa 033 de 2020**, unified incident taxonomy and standardized security metrics and incident reporting, developed from Circular Externa 007 de 2018.
- US Department of Justice (DOJ). **Policy regarding charging decisions under the Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA)**, May 2022.
